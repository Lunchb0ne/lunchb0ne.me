# Spotlib

Spotlib is a small web app that connects to your Spotify account and shows your
listening habits — top tracks, top artists, and recommendations based on what
you already like.

## Why I built it

I wanted a quick way to see what I'd been listening to without digging through
Spotify's own stats, and it was a good excuse to learn the Spotify API.

## How it works

The app authenticates with Spotify, pulls your top tracks and artists over a few
time ranges, and uses the recommendations endpoint to suggest new music. The UI
is built with React and TypeScript, with a few Framer Motion animations to make
transitions feel smooth.

## What I learned

Working with OAuth and rate limits was the trickiest part. It pushed me to think
more carefully about caching responses and handling errors gracefully.
