# dйcraft

dйcraft was my way of getting hands-on with WebGL. It's an interactive 3D scene
you can move around, built mostly to learn how shaders work.

## Why I built it

I'd been curious about Three.js and GLSL for a while. Rather than read about them,
I gave myself a small project to actually ship something.

## How it works

The scene runs on Three.js inside a Next.js app. I wrote a few custom GLSL
shaders for the lighting and surface effects, and spent a lot of time tuning
performance so it stayed smooth on lower-end devices.

## What I learned

Shaders have a steep learning curve, but they're worth it. I came away with a much
better understanding of how the GPU renders a frame and how to keep things fast.
