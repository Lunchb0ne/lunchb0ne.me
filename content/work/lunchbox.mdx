# Lunchbox

Lunchbox is a recipe app I built to make browsing and reading recipes feel less
cluttered than most cooking sites.

## Why I built it

Most recipe sites bury the actual recipe under ads and long intros. I wanted a
simple, readable alternative — and a project to try out Nuxt and a headless CMS.

## How it works

Recipes are managed in Sanity CMS, so content is easy to edit without touching
code. The frontend is built with Vue and Nuxt, with a strong focus on typography
and layout so recipes are easy to follow while you cook.

## What I learned

This was my first time using a headless CMS, and it made me appreciate keeping
content and code separate. It also pushed me to think harder about typographic
rhythm and readability.
