# Portfolio

This was the first version of my personal site, built before I moved everything
over to Next.js.

## Why I built it

I needed somewhere to put my work, and I wanted to keep it simple — no frameworks,
just the basics done well.

## How it works

The whole thing is hand-written HTML, CSS, and a little vanilla JavaScript. No
build step, no dependencies. It loaded fast and was easy to maintain.

## What I learned

Building without a framework was a good reminder of the fundamentals. A lot of
what I learned here carried over when I rebuilt the site with Next.js.
